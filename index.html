About

A collection of experiments in cellular automata, generative grammar, math tooling, computing machines and playful interfaces. Everything runs entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and no account or install is required.

All tools are free software under GPL-3.0 and can be downloaded as a single self-contained HTML file for offline use.
