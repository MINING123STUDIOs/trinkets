Snakes & Ladders
Classic board game for 2-4 players on a 10×10 grid (squares 1-100).

Roll: Click the button to roll a die (1-6). Move your token forward that many squares.
Ladders: Land on a ladder base and climb up! (green path)
Snakes: Land on a snake head and slide down! (red path)
Winning: First to reach square 100 wins. By default you must land on 100 exactly — overshooting stays put.
Bounce toggle: When enabled, overshooting bounces you back (e.g. 95 + 6 = 101 → square 1).
AI: Set a player to AI and they roll automatically.
