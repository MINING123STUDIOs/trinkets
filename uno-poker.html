Uno Poker
Poker-style betting with a 108-card Uno deck (R/G/B/Y × 0-9 + reverse/+2/skip + 4 Wild + 4 Draw 4).

Deal: 2 hand cards per player. 5 community cards revealed in 3+1 stages with betting between each.
Matching: Wild (★) and Draw 4 (+4) match everything. Other cards match by same color or same symbol (number/r/+2/skip).
Betting: Check, Call, Raise or Fold each round. Last player standing wins the pot.
Winning: Most matches between hand (2 cards) and community (5 cards) wins. Ties carry the pot forward.
